# Solar Cell Output Prediction (Matlab and Python)

The prediction of solar power generation systems is a very active research field, as reliable information about future power generation can ensure the safe operation of the grid and help to minimize operational costs. Deep learning algorithms have been proven to be very powerful in prediction tasks, such as economic time series or speech recognition. So far, deep learning algorithms have rarely been used for predicting renewable energy power plants. By using different deep learning algorithms, such as RNN, LSTM with different feature extraction methods, such as PCA, 1D CNN, autoencoders, my motivation is to demonstrate the predictive strength of these algorithms compared to standard MLP and traditional machine learning models when predicting the energy output of 21 solar power plants.
Possible solutions:
Apply different deep learning techniques, such as MLP, Simple RNN, LSTM, GRU, etc., on solar power generation systems to predict solar power generation based on specific weather conditions at the location of the solar power generation system.
